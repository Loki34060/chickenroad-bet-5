How the crossing mechanic works

Instead of a pegboard, chickenroad style gameplay usually uses a lane or path with a series of safe tiles and danger points. Each step forward is a fresh random outcome, and the longer you stay in play, the higher the potential payout grows. That is the basic idea behind the chicken road betting game: steady progression with rising tension, but no guarantee that the next move will be safe.

The best way to think about it is as a sequence of choices rather than a race. Once the run begins, the result of each step is set by the game logic, not by your timing or button rhythm. The result is a simple loop that suits players who want quick rounds without learning complex rules.
